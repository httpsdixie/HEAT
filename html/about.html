ORCHID

Ormoc City Heat Index Device

ORCHID is a real-time environmental heat index monitoring system developed for the City Government of Ormoc. It collects temperature, humidity, pressure, and heat index readings from deployed sensor nodes across the city to support public health advisories and climate analysis.
